## Who is it for?

It is aimed at <span class="highlight">early childhood educators</span> (preschool teachers, classroom assistants, home-based educators and childcare staff) who work with children from birth to 5 years old. It is an inclusive training, useful for working with:

- Children with typical development.
- Children with language delays or difficulties.
- Children who are learning a second language (bilingual).

## Duration and frequency

The full programme consists of <span class="highlight">3 independent modules</span> (Trainings A, B and C). Each module lasts 7 hours, adding up to <span class="highlight">21 hours</span> of professional development if all are taken.

## What do we work on?

The programme divides learning into three main pillars, using practical workbooks and the analysis of real videos:

- <span class="highlight">Module A: Promoting language development in early childhood settings:</span> This is the foundation of the programme. It focuses on how educators can interact so that children communicate more.
- <span class="highlight">Module B: Letting language lead the way to literacy:</span> It focuses on emergent literacy and on preparing the ground before primary school.
- <span class="highlight">Module C: Promoting peer interaction in early childhood settings:</span> It targets social skills and inclusion within the group directly.
