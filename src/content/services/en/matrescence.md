## Who is it for?

- Women in the <span class="highlight">preconception</span> stage or trying to get pregnant.
- <span class="highlight">Pregnant women</span> who want to prepare psychologically for the change.
- Mothers in the <span class="highlight">early or late postpartum</span> period (including the first years of parenting).
- Mothers who feel <span class="highlight">ambivalence, loss of identity, guilt or emotional overwhelm</span> and need a judgment-free space.

## Duration and frequency

Sessions usually last <span class="highlight">between 50 and 60 minutes</span>. They are usually <span class="highlight">weekly or every two weeks</span>, adapting to the mother's pace and needs, and the whole process can last <span class="highlight">a few months</span> depending on each case.

## What do we work on?

The content adapts to each woman's experience, but it is generally built around the following pillars:

- **Validating maternal ambivalence:** A space to express contradictory feelings (such as deep love for the baby alongside nostalgia for the life before) without guilt.
- **Rebuilding identity:** Support in grieving the "woman before" and in the birth of the "identity as a mother".
- **Expectations vs. reality:** Looking at social pressure and the myths of the "perfect mother", and how to build a real motherhood of your own.
- **Emotional regulation and self-care:** Practical tools to manage stress, chronic tiredness and mental load.
- **Support network and partner:** Reviewing the new couple dynamic (if there is one) and strategies to communicate needs assertively to those around you.
