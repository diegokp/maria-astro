## Who is it for?

This course is designed for mothers, fathers, guardians and primary caregivers of <span class="highlight">children of any age</span>, especially those who:

- Feel stress, overwhelm or burnout from parenting.
- Tend to react with shouting, anger or impulsiveness and want to respond more calmly.
- Want to improve emotional connection and communication with their children.
- Want to break automatic or inherited parenting patterns and parent from a place of respect.

## Duration and frequency

The standard programme usually lasts <span class="highlight">8 weeks</span>, with <span class="highlight">one 2–2.5-hour group session a week</span> and <span class="highlight">15–20 minutes</span> of daily practice at home. Intensive weekend workshops and 4-week introductory workshops are also offered.

## What do we work on?

- **Mapping autopilot:** Identifying how we react unconsciously to family stress and learning to "pause" before acting.
- **Beginner's mind in parenting:** Learning to see our children as they are in the present moment, letting go of labels, expectations and judgments from the past.
- **Stress management and self-regulation:** Breathing and body-awareness techniques to calm our own nervous system when a child has a tantrum or behaves in a challenging way.
- **Acceptance and compassion:** Learning to treat ourselves kindly when we make parenting mistakes (self-compassion) and to accept our child's limits.
- **Conflict and repair:** Strategies to resolve family disagreements assertively and to repair the emotional bond after a moment of tension.
