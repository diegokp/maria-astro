## Who is it for?

The service is tailored to three specific age groups, adapting the methodology to each:

- **Children:** Young people who need to channel their emotions or overcome difficulties through play therapy (where play is their natural language).
- **Adolescents:** Young people going through identity crises, academic stress, self-esteem issues or learning to manage their independence.
- **Adults:** People looking for Mindfulness tools to manage anxiety and work or personal stress, or to improve their self-regulation.

## Duration and frequency

Individual sessions usually last <span class="highlight">55 minutes</span>. For young children, direct screen time may be shorter or combined with parent guidance to avoid digital fatigue.

We usually start with <span class="highlight">one session a week</span> to build the therapeutic relationship and assess the case. Later, depending on progress and the goals reached, sessions can be spaced out to <span class="highlight">every two weeks</span>.

## What do we work on?

### In childhood (Play Therapy)

- Expressing trauma, fears or frustrations in an indirect and safe way.
- Processing life changes (moving house, divorce, grief).
- Developing emotional intelligence and reducing disruptive behaviour.

### In adolescence

- Building healthy self-esteem and handling social pressure.
- Coping tools for anxiety and uncertainty about the future.
- Assertive communication with parents and managing the school environment.

### In adulthood (Mindfulness)

- Reducing chronic stress and managing rumination (repetitive thoughts).
- Connecting with the present moment to break impulsive or anxious reaction patterns.
- Developing self-compassion and greater day-to-day emotional stability.
