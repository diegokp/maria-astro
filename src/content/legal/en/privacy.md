## Why do we process your personal data and on what basis?

### Contact form

The contact form collects your first name, last name, e-mail address and the message you write. We will process them confidentially in order to:

- Reply to the enquiries, appointment requests or requests for information about courses and services that you make through the form or any of the contact methods available on this website. *(Based on your consent, art. 6.1.a GDPR, and to take pre-contractual steps at your request, art. 6.1.b GDPR)*

If your message includes information about your health or your children's health, it will be processed solely to answer your enquiry, on the basis of your explicit consent (art. 9.2.a GDPR). We recommend not including detailed sensitive information in the form; you can share it in the first session.

If you subscribe to the newsletter, your first name (optional) and e-mail address are collected to send you information about courses, services and news. *(Based on your consent, art. 6.1.a GDPR)* You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every e-mail.
